What we do

Four engagements. Each one ends in a document you can put in front of somebody.

Diligence

Counterparty & Network Diligence

Who you are about to take money from, or hand money to, in a jurisdiction where the registry lies. Ownership, offshore structures, sanctions exposure, family networks, and a clean-or-compromised call you can act on.

Scope & deliverable
Damages

Forensic Damages Quantification

A defensible dollar figure for an extraction claim, historical or live. Model, assumptions, ranges, sensitivity, and the source apparatus that lets a hostile expert check every step and fail to break it.

Succession

Which institution alive today inherits a dead entity's obligations. Corporate genealogy with documentary basis... and the false chains killed, because a claim built on a bad succession dies on the first motion.
