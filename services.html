Diligence

Counterparty & Network Diligence

Haiti's corporate registry is not a reliable account of who owns what. Neither is a press release. We reconstruct the actual network: beneficial ownership, offshore vehicles, sanctions and enforcement exposure, family and marriage ties, import and arms concessions, and the funding history of the entity in front of you.

You get a call, not a hedge. If a partner is clean we say clean and show why. If a partner is compromised we show the structure and name the exposure.

DeliverableEntity dossier with network map, offshore and sanctions screening, source log, and a written clean/compromised determination.
ForFunders, INGOs, impact investors, diaspora capital, anyone signing with a Haitian counterparty.
SourcesOFAC SDN, ICIJ Offshore Leaks (494K entities, 3.5M relationships), GDELT, court and registry filings, primary reporting.
Typical2–4 weeks for a single entity. Network engagements scope longer.
Damages

Forensic Damages Quantification

The hardest question in a reparations or extraction claim is not whether harm happened. It is what the harm is worth, in a figure that a finance ministry, a court, or an opposing economist cannot laugh out of the room.

We build the model from primary financial records, state the assumptions in the open, run the ranges, and publish the sensitivity. Nothing is asserted that cannot be traced back to a document.

DeliverableDamages report: model specification, low/central/high ranges, sensitivity analysis, full source apparatus, and an exhibit-ready summary table.
ForPlaintiff counsel, states, truth commissions, reparations bodies, policy shops.
MethodEEDTM / EDTM. Elite capture rate validated across 25+ cases with three independent derivations.
Typical6–12 weeks depending on archive access.
Succession

Successor Liability Tracing

A 200-year-old claim is worthless if it points at a bank that dissolved in 1848. It is worth a great deal if it points at one that reports quarterly. We trace the chain of succession through mergers, liquidations, recapitalizations, and nationalizations until it terminates in an entity with a balance sheet.

We also kill chains. On the Haiti file we retired a widely repeated Laffitte-to-CIC succession that does not survive the dates, and rebuilt the real exposure through CIC's own 1875 refinancing. Publishing the correction is part of the product.

DeliverableChain-of-succession memo: corporate genealogy, documentary basis at each hop, surviving entity identification, and a corrections ledger of chains ruled out.
ForLitigators, commissions, investigative desks, campaign strategists.
OutputNamed defendants with modeled exposure, ranked by evidentiary strength and jurisdictional reach.
Typical4–8 weeks per institution.
