The 1825 Indemnity and the 1914 Gold Seizure

France recognized Haitian independence in 1825 in exchange for an indemnity Haiti borrowed to pay, then serviced for over a century. In 1914 a US bank's agents removed Haiti's gold reserve to New York under Marine escort. Both events are documented. Neither had a modern liability figure attached to a surviving defendant.

We quantified both, traced the lending syndicate forward through 200 years of French banking consolidation, added the Caisse des Dépôts as a third defendant, and retired a false succession chain that had circulated for years.

The 1914 seizure has never been litigated. US corporate defendant, SDNY jurisdiction, armed-robbery facts on the record.

PetroCaribe Forensic Ledger

Haiti's PetroCaribe funds are the most discussed corruption file in the country and one of the least usable, because the public numbers do not agree with each other and the audited totals get quoted as if they were the disbursed totals.

We rebuilt the ledger from the underlying records and produced defendant tables mapping named individuals and entities to specific amounts, including a shell identity that appears twice under two names.

The correct figures are $2,238,164,040.74 voted and $1,605,905,287.84 disbursed. The widely circulated $1.94B is a coverage denominator, not a disbursement.

Status Termination Evidence Portfolio

When protected status for 350,000 Haitians was terminated, the litigation channel narrowed sharply and the equities argument became the surviving lane. That argument needs numbers: what termination costs, who bears it, and what the record shows about how the decision was made.

We built the cost model, ran the enforcement data, produced the fact-sheet set and the compliance analysis, and compiled the whole thing into an indexed portfolio for counsel.

Counterparty Screening, Cité Soleil

A community organization in Cité Soleil was a candidate partner for a large electrification deployment. The question from the capital side was blunt: is this organization funded or controlled by any of the families that dominate Haitian commerce and appear in the arms and gang files?

We ran the funding history, the board, the network, and the offshore and sanctions exposure of every adjacent entity.

Determination: clean. The deployment moved forward, with a separate finding on how to route capital without tripping the terrorist-designation problem that had been blocking it.

Method

Why the Haiti numbers hold

The Haiti figures are not one-off estimates. They come out of a model tested against every other extraction case we could document, across four continents and two centuries, before it was ever pointed at Haiti.

The finding underneath it: elite institutions capture roughly 80 percent of extracted value regardless of the mechanism, the century, or the country. That rate has three independent derivations from unrelated literatures, which is the reason it survives cross-examination.
